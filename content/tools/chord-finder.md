## How to find a piano chord

Tap the piano keys or enter notes such as **C E G**. You can also enter notes with octaves, such as **E3 G3 C4**, or MIDI note numbers, such as **60 64 67**. When you leave out the octaves, the finder puts the notes in ascending order as you type them. With octaves or MIDI numbers, the lowest pitch determines the bass.

Play **E–G–C** and you’ll see **C/E**, a C major chord with E in the bass. Some note sets have several names. For example, **C–E–G–A** can be **C6** or **Am7/C**. The song’s key and surrounding chords can help you decide which name fits.

## How to look up a chord

Choose **Find keys**, then enter a chord symbol such as **Fmaj7**, **B♭m9** or **C/E**. Use the inversion menu to change which chord tone is lowest. Press **Play chord** to hear the notes together.
