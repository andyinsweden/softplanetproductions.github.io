<section class="sibelius-section" id="sibelius-services">
  <h2 id="sibelius-licence-options">Compare Sibelius Ultimate site licence options</h2>
  <p class="sibelius-section-lead">A Sibelius site licence lets a school or college purchase multiple seats for its music department. Avid sets a five-seat minimum for a new site licence. The right setup depends on where students and staff use Sibelius and how many need it at the same time.</p>
  <div class="sibelius-services">
    <article>
      <h3>Standalone site licences</h3>
      <p>Choose standalone seats for designated computers that need their own activation, such as teaching workstations or computers in separate rooms.</p>
    </article>
    <article>
      <h3>Network site licences</h3>
      <p>A central Sibelius Licence Server shares a pool of seats across connected computers. Size the pool for the largest number of people who need to run Sibelius at the same time.</p>
    </article>
    <article>
      <h3>Single-user licences</h3>
      <p>For fewer than five seats, Avid directs new customers to single-user licences. Tell us which computers or staff need access and we can discuss the options.</p>
    </article>
    <article>
      <h3>Additional seats and upgrades</h3>
      <p>Adding a classroom or upgrading an older version? We can review your existing licence type, version and seat count. Some older licences need an upgrade before you can add seats.</p>
    </article>
  </div>
  <p class="mt-6">See <a class="sibelius-text-link" href="https://kb.avid.com/pkb/articles/en_US/Knowledge/Purchasing-multi-seat-licences?popup=true">Avid’s Sibelius Ultimate multi-seat licensing guide</a> for current options.</p>
</section>

  <h2>What to include in your Sibelius site licence enquiry</h2>
  <ul class="list-disc pl-6">
    <li>Your school or college name and contact details.</li>
    <li>The number of computers involved and the maximum number of people using Sibelius at once.</li>
    <li>Whether you use Windows, Mac or both, and whether IT manages the computers centrally.</li>
